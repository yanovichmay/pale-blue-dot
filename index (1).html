"Pale Blue Dot" is a famous photograph of planet Earth taken by NASA's Voyager 1 space probe on February 14, 1990.

Captured from a record distance of over 6 billion kilometers, Earth appears as a tiny, fragile speck (just 0.12 pixels) within a beam of scattered sunlight.

The image was taken at the request of astronomer Carl Sagan, who used it to remind humanity of our planet's vulnerability and our responsibility to cherish our only home.
